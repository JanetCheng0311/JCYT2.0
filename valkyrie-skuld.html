VALKYRIE-SKULD

An original sci-fi robot character, taken end-to-end from concept to a final rendered video.

YouTube ↗ Behance ↗

Overview

VALKYRIE-SKULD is an original science-fiction robot character developed as a complete production pipeline — from initial concept design through modeling, materials and real-time integration to a final video presentation.

Process

  • Concept design for the character
  • Modeling in Blender
  • Retopology to a clean, animation-ready mesh
  • PBR material authoring
  • Integration into Unreal Engine
  • Animation
  • Final video presentation

Materials & pipeline

The asset was built around a game-ready pipeline: a detailed model in Blender, retopologised for clean topology, then surfaced with physically based rendering (PBR) materials and brought into Unreal Engine for real-time rendering.

Tools

Blender Unreal Engine PBR Materials Retopology Animation

Outcome

Delivered as a final video presentation, with the full project documented on Behance.
